Studio Methodology

The Five-Stage Process

A deliberate, unhurried workflow designed to eliminate guesswork and translate the high caliber of your beauty practice into an effortless, revenue-generating digital system.

Sprint Track · 2 Weeks

Boutique & Solo Specialist

Ideal for independent hairstylists, skin specialists, and boutique lash/nail artists seeking immediate digital prestige and seamless direct booking.

Flagship Track · 4 to 6 Weeks

Clinic & Multi-Chair Salon

For established aesthetic clinics, medspas, and multi-staff salons requiring comprehensive brand positioning, deep treatment menus, and local search visibility.

STAGE 01 Week 1

DISCOVER

We immerse ourselves in your beauty business: your physical atmosphere, your signature treatments, your pricing structure, your client demographics, and where your current booking journey leaks revenue.

• In-depth brand and competitor audit
• Client booking friction analysis
• Treatment pricing and category review
• Technical and domain inventory
STAGE 02 Week 1 to 2

STRATEGIZE

We architect the digital blueprint: the exact structure of your treatment menus, your scheduling workflow (connecting Fresha, Phorest, Vagaro, or establishing a direct booking flow), and local search keyword mapping.

• Information architecture and page wireframes
• Booking software workflow integration
• Local high-intent SEO keyword mapping
• Treatment menu hierarchy blueprint
STAGE 03 Week 2 to 3

CREATE

We craft the visual identity, typography, editorial layouts, and web interface. Every layout is presented with high-definition styling and tactile beauty aesthetics that reflect the physical luxury of your space.

• Bespoke typography and visual styling
• Mobile-first editorial layouts
• Custom treatment card design
• Private interactive staging preview
STAGE 04 Week 3 to 4

LAUNCH

Rigorous quality assurance across iPhone, Android, tablets, and desktop displays. We connect custom domains, install SSL, test real booking transactions, and tune speed to perfection.

• Cross-device mobile and retina QA
• Live booking and deposit end-to-end test
• Core Web Vitals speed optimization
• Zero-downtime domain launch
STAGE 05 Ongoing

GROW

We ensure your business maintains commercial momentum. Local search indexing, Google Business Profile enhancements, social content direction, and recurring client retention systems.

• Google search indexation verification
• Local map ranking optimization
• Social bio and campaign alignment
• Monthly support and technical care
