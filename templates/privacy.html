Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 2026 · QUORV Brand Studio

1. Introduction

QUORV Brand Studio (“QUORV”, “we”, “our”, or “us”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal data of our website visitors, clients, and prospective beauty partners. This policy explains how we collect, store, and process your information when you browse our website, message us via WhatsApp, or submit an inquiry.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information only to communicate regarding brand design, web architecture, and digital services for beauty businesses:

  • Inquiry Details: Name, business name, email address, phone/WhatsApp number, current website, business sector, and project requirements.
  • Communication Records: Correspondence conducted via email or WhatsApp regarding scoping, timelines, and proposals.
  • Anonymous Usage Data: Technical diagnostic details (browser type, referral source, interaction events) to maintain website security and user experience. We do not sell or monetize tracking data.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your data is processed strictly for legitimate business purposes:

  • To respond to your inquiries and evaluate your beauty business requirements.
  • To prepare bespoke project proposals, quotes, and technical specifications.
  • To fulfill studio contractual obligations and provide ongoing technical support.
  • To comply with applicable UK and international commercial legal obligations.

4. WhatsApp and Third-Party Platforms

When you communicate with us via WhatsApp, communication is additionally subject to Meta/WhatsApp’s privacy policies. We do not use your phone number for unsolicited marketing broadcasts or automated SMS spam.

5. Data Retention & Security

We retain inquiry information only as long as necessary to conduct ongoing business dialogues or satisfy accounting obligations. All data stored within our database and administrative tools is safeguarded with modern encryption and access controls.

6. Your Rights

Under UK GDPR and applicable data protection legislation, you hold the right to request access to, rectification of, or deletion of your personal data held by QUORV. To exercise these rights, email us directly at quorv911@gmail.com.
